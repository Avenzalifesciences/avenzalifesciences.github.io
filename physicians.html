Physicians & Investigators

Avenza Life Sciences offers a physician-centric research model that enables investigators to participate in clinical trials without disrupting routine clinical care—while maintaining compliance, quality, and operational clarity.

Physician-Led Research, SMO-Managed Operations

We function as your operational backbone—handling the study processes so physicians can focus on patient care and appropriate clinical oversight.

How We Support Physicians

  • Study start-up planning, feasibility coordination, and activation readiness
  • IRB submissions, amendments, continuing review, and regulatory maintenance
  • Patient identification, prescreening, enrollment, and visit coordination
  • Training coordination and essential document readiness across the study lifecycle
  • EDC setup and workflow support (e.g., CRIO, Medpace, REDCap, study-specific systems)
  • Bridge communication between Sponsors, CROs, sites, and patients to reduce burden
  • Close-out preparation and documentation support aligned with ICH-GCP expectations

Operational Benefits

  • Reduced administrative burden and fewer operational disruptions
  • Increased trial access and sustainable research participation
  • Improved study readiness through SOP-driven processes
  • Additional revenue opportunities with structured trial participation
