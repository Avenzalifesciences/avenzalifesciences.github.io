How The System Decides

The actual pipeline, in order, with the real thresholds it runs on

  1. 1

    Scan the market

    Combines a fixed universe of ~50 liquid stocks across sectors with today's actual top gainers/losers/most-active pulled live from Alpaca's screener — then drops any of those movers that have no listed options at all, since this system only trades options.

  2. 2

    Score the watchlist

    Each candidate is scored on volume spikes, momentum vs. its 20-day average, a volatility squeeze setup, sector leadership, and whether it's an actual mover today. Top 10 make the cut.

  3. 3

    Decide: call, put, straddle, or sit out

    Weighted technical indicators + sentiment vote on direction. "No trade" is the default — a trade only fires when the combined confidence clears the bar.

    min confidence to trade: 0.35 sentiment weight: 25% min indicator hit-rate: 52%
  4. 4

    Allocate capital by priority

    Highest-confidence candidates get funded first. A reserve is always held back for a better setup later, sector concentration is capped, and a symbol already held won't get funded again.

    reserve held back: 25% max per sector: 2 positions max per symbol: 1 open position
  5. 5

    Pick a contract and price the order

    Nearest at-the-money contract, 10-18 days to expiration. Never a raw market order — a marketable limit when momentum is urgent, a midpoint limit otherwise, and it skips the trade entirely if the spread is too wide to trade safely.

    expiration window: 10-18 days urgent above ADX 40 max spread: 15% of mid
  6. 6

    Monitor and exit

    Every open position is re-checked every 10 minutes, in this priority order: forced exit inside 1 day of expiration, conditional exit inside 4 days if no longer favorable, immediate exit if the entry signal reverses, stop-loss hit, then partial profit-taking at a trailing target. Nothing is marked closed or reduced until the closing order is actually confirmed filled at the broker.
