About Avenza Life Sciences

Avenza Life Sciences is a Site Management Organization (SMO) dedicated to advancing clinical research through structured, compliant, and physician-led site operations.

Who We Are

Avenza Life Sciences supports the execution of clinical trials across multiple therapeutic areas by partnering with Sponsors, Contract Research Organizations (CROs), Physicians, and Patients.

Our operating model integrates clinical research into routine healthcare settings, enabling physician-led research at point-of-care sites. This approach improves patient access to studies, accelerates enrollment timelines, and ensures clinical oversight aligned with ICH-GCP principles.

By centralizing site management activities, Avenza reduces operational burden on investigator sites while maintaining high standards of data integrity, patient safety, and regulatory compliance.

Our Operating Model

  • Centralized oversight of site operations across the study lifecycle
  • Standardized SOP-driven workflows aligned with ICH-GCP guidelines
  • Physician-led patient enrollment and clinical monitoring
  • Integrated regulatory, operational, and data management support
  • Inspection-ready documentation and continuous compliance focus

Our Mission

Our mission is to enable high-quality clinical research by providing reliable, efficient, and compliant site operations that support scientific advancement and patient-centered care.

Avenza Life Sciences is committed to delivering predictable execution, transparent communication, and ethical research conduct across all studies we support.
